# Traackly walkthrough

A slow, narrated walkthrough that follows one customer order from the order desk to the shop floor. The order changes after approval, and you see that change caught, flagged and acknowledged before the job is finished.

## About the recording

The walkthrough was recorded against the real running app with seeded demo data; nothing in it is a mock-up. The narration is a synthetic English voice generated on the recording machine, and the on-screen captions match it word for word.
